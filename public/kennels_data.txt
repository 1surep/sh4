RULES
In the Hash, it is believed that there are no rules but there are traditions. However there exists certain basic incontrovertible rules which are universal. These rules are more like the foundation of hashing and were established by the originators of the Hash as far back as 1938. These rules are:
1.	The Grand Master/Hash Master is always right.
2.	When in doubt as to the actions of the Grand Master/Hash Master, then refer to Rule 1.
Some Hash Houses have actually created new rules to guide the conduct of hashers. However, in all such cases, Rule 1 still applies. 

TRADITIONS
•	Several Traditions exist in the Hash and violations of such traditions are likely to invoke a punishment such as ‘sitting on ice’ which can be accompanied by a ‘Fuck-off’ or a ‘Piss-Off’ depending on the severity of the violation.
•	A Down-Down is one of the oldest traditions of the Hash and is a means of either punishing or rewarding a hasher for offences or actions ‘real or imagined’. Remember, the GM/HM is always right and cannot be argued with.
•	The traditional drink of the Hash is Beer. Therefore, in every Hash Circle ALL drinks/beverages are generally referred to as Beer. It is also important to note that ‘Beer’ is generally consumed with the left hand especially in Hash Circles. Drinking with your right hand can be considered an infraction.
•	Traditionally, new shoes are not allowed in the Hash. A Hasher who is caught with new shoes in a Hash run is to drink from the shoes.
•	No headgear, spectacles or sunglasses are to be worn when giving or receiving a Down-Down.
•	A Hasher who wishes to exit or enter a Hash Circle is required to place their drinking mug on their head and signal whoever is in charge of the Circle at the time.
•	A Hasher being given a Down-Down is required to FINISH the drink and place the mug upside down on the head. This must be done whether you finish the drink or not.
•	When in charge of a Hash Circle, never point with fingers, rather bend arm and direct elbow towards the person.
•	Do not Talk while in the Circle unless called upon to do so.
•	Never refuse to sit on ice when punished regardless of your believed innocence. Remember, the GM/HM is always right.
